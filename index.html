Questions

Good to know

Are you a bank or a lender?

No. Bespoke Mortgage Advisory is an advisory service. We do not provide financing. The loan is offered and approved by the bank you choose.

Who do you help?

Malaysian homebuyers and property owners, whether you are buying your first home, upgrading, or reviewing an existing loan.

Can you guarantee my loan will be approved?

No one honestly can. Approval is the bank's decision. What we do is assess your position properly beforehand and help you submit a well-prepared application.

I am a property agent. How do I refer a client?

Message us on WhatsApp using the "Partner with us" button. We will explain how we work together and take it from there.
